# Strada Intelligence — Dashboard Guide

*A plain-English walkthrough of every number on the dashboard and what it tells you.*

Strada Intelligence is a Dubai property market tracker. It pulls together live transaction data, rental yields, asking prices, regional news, and global financial markets into a single screen — then turns all of it into one simple answer: **is now a good time to buy, hold, or hold off in Dubai property?**

The dashboard is built to be read top to bottom in a couple of minutes. This guide explains each section in the same order it appears on screen.

---

## 1. The Verdict — the one-line answer

**What it shows:** A single score from **0 to 5**, plus a plain-English label such as *"Strong Market"* and a short recommendation.

**What it means:** This is the headline takeaway. It combines all the signals on the dashboard into one number so a busy investor can glance once and know where the market stands. Higher is healthier.

**How it's built:** The verdict is a weighted blend of seven underlying market forces (the "pillars" in Section 3). Each force is scored, and the more important ones count for more. Roughly:

| Force | Weight |
|---|---|
| Security & geopolitics | 22% |
| Oil & Gulf wealth | 15% |
| UAE & Gulf stock markets | 12% |
| Global interest rates | 12% |
| Foreign buyer demand | 10% |
| Dubai transaction activity | 10% |
| Tourism & aviation | 8% |
| Property market sentiment | 7% |

The combined score is then matched to a recommendation band — for example, a high score reads as *"Most signals are healthy — a good time to hold and invest,"* while a very low score flips to a defensive *"cash only"* stance.

It also shows an **"as of" date** so you always know how fresh the reading is.

---

## 2. The Four Headline Numbers (KPIs)

These are the four big tiles across the top. Each shows a current value and how it has changed, colour-coded green (up) or red (down).

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Properties Sold** | How many property transactions completed in Dubai over the last 7 days, and whether that's up or down versus the previous week. A proxy for how busy and liquid the market is right now. |
| **Total Sales Value** | The total money (in AED billions) that changed hands in those transactions over the last week. Shows whether the *money* in the market is growing, not just the number of deals. |
| **Average PSF** | The average price **per square foot** paid across the market, shown as a 30-day average. This is the cleanest way to track whether prices themselves are rising or falling, independent of how big the properties are. |
| **Rental Yield** | The average **gross annual rental yield** — the rent a property earns in a year as a percentage of its price. The core number for any investor: it shows how much income the property generates. |

Each tile also carries a short comparison label (e.g. *"vs last week"* or *"30-day avg"*) so the change is never read out of context.

---

## 3. What's Driving the Market — The Seven Pillars

**What it shows:** Seven forces that move Dubai property prices, each scored **out of 5** and explained in one sentence. Green means the force is *helping* property values; red means it's adding *pressure*.

This is the analytical heart of the product. Rather than asking the investor to follow oil prices, interest rates, geopolitics and stock markets themselves, the system researches each one, scores it, and explains it in plain English.

| Pillar | The question it answers |
|---|---|
| **Is the Region Stable?** | Are conflicts or instability affecting investor confidence in the Gulf? |
| **Gulf Oil Wealth** | Do oil-rich Gulf states have money flowing in to invest in Dubai property? |
| **Dubai Company Health** | Are Dubai's biggest developers (e.g. Emaar, DAMAC) financially strong? |
| **Are Mortgages Affordable?** | Are global and local interest rates working for or against buyers? |
| **Foreign Buyer Appetite** | Are international buyers (India, China, Russia, etc.) still active? |
| **Tourism & People Moving** | Is Dubai still growing as a place people visit and move to? |
| **Property Market Mood** | How are buyers and sellers actually feeling — urgency, time-on-market, asking vs. achieved prices? |

Each pillar carries a one-line summary of the *why* behind its score (for example: *"Brent at $82 — comfortably above the floor; sovereign wealth deployment continues"*). Together they make the headline Verdict transparent: an investor can see exactly which forces are pushing the score up and which are dragging it down.

---

## 4. Property Mix — What Kind of Property Is Selling?

**What it shows:** A breakdown of recent sales by property type — apartments, villas, townhouses, penthouses (or, alternatively, off-plan vs. completed "secondary" homes).

**What it means:** Tells you where demand is concentrated. A market dominated by apartments behaves very differently from one where villas and off-plan launches are driving activity. Helps an investor understand *what* the market is buying, not just how much.

---

## 5. The 9-Month Trend Charts

**What it shows:** Three small line charts tracking the last several months of:

- **Average PSF** — the price-per-square-foot trend over time.
- **Volume** — the number of transactions over time.
- **Yield** — the rental yield trend over time.

**What it means:** The headline tiles (Section 2) show *today's* number; these charts show the *direction of travel*. They answer "is this a blip or a trend?" A rising price line with rising volume is a strong, healthy market; rising prices on falling volume is an early warning.

---

## 6. Average Asking Price Per Square Foot

**What it shows:** The current asking price per square foot, split by **Apartments** and **Villas**, each with its recent percentage change.

**What it means:** While Section 2's "Average PSF" reflects what buyers actually *paid*, this reflects what sellers are currently *asking*. Comparing the two reveals how much room there is to negotiate and whether sellers are getting more or less confident.

---

## 7. Transaction Volume by Area

**What it shows:** A ranked league table of Dubai's most active neighbourhoods over the last 7 days — Dubai Marina, Downtown, JVC, Business Bay, and so on — each with its transaction volume, price per square foot, and a trend arrow.

**What it means:** Shows *where the money is moving*. An investor can instantly see which communities are hottest this week and how their pricing compares, helping target where to buy.

---

## 8. Rental Yield by Property Type

**What it shows:** Gross annual rental yield broken down by unit type — studio, 1-bed, 2-bed, 3-bed, villa, penthouse.

**What it means:** Yield almost always falls as properties get bigger and more expensive. This section makes that trade-off explicit, so an income-focused investor can see that (for example) a studio earning 7.4% throws off far more income relative to price than a penthouse at 3.8%.

---

## 9. Recent Sales Transactions

**What it shows:** A live feed of the most recent individual sales — date, area, building, unit type, price, and price per square foot.

**What it means:** This is the raw evidence under the averages. It lets an investor sanity-check the headline numbers against real, named deals and spot specific comparable sales in buildings they care about.
